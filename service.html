Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a project with you?
You can reach out through the contact form or email me directly with your project details. We'll schedule a quick discussion to understand your requirements, goals, and timeline before starting.
How long does a typical project take?
Project timelines depend on the scope and complexity. A standard landing page or simple website usually takes 1-2 weeks, while a full-stack web app or complex system may take 3-6 weeks.
What are your payment terms?
I usually work with a 50% upfront deposit to kickstart the project, and the remaining 50% upon final completion and full approval before final delivery.
Do you offer post-launch support?
Yes, I provide free bug-fix support for 14-30 days after launch. After that, you can choose one of my monthly maintenance & support plans for ongoing updates.
Will I own the code and intellectual property?
Yes, 100%. Once the final payment is cleared, full ownership of the source code, design assets, and intellectual property will be transferred to you.
