Personal Biography

Passionate About Building What Matters

My journey into technology started at age 14 when I built my first website on GeoCities. What began as curiosity evolved into a career spanning front-end engineering, back-end architecture, cloud infrastructure, and product design.

After graduating with a B.Sc. in Computer Science, I joined a digital agency where I led development for 30+ client projects. Later, I transitioned to freelancing — which gave me the freedom to choose meaningful work and build long-term partnerships with clients across four continents.

Outside of screens, I mentor junior developers through an online community, write about design systems on Medium, and occasionally contribute to open-source projects. I believe the best code is the code that serves people well.
